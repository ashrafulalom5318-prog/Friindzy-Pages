01 — Scope

Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Friindzy mobile application, website, and related services that link to this Policy (collectively, the “Service”).

This Policy should be read together with the Friindzy Terms of Use. If you use a third-party AI provider, that provider may independently process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

02 — Information

Information We Collect

The information collected by Friindzy depends on which features you use. Depending on the implementation of your account and the Service, this may include:

  • Account information such as your name, email address, or authentication identifiers.
  • Conversation information, including prompts, messages, responses, and conversation metadata.
  • Files, documents, images, code, and other content you choose to upload or create.
  • Custom Instructions and personalization settings you choose to save.
  • API-provider configuration and connected API credentials where BYOK is enabled.
  • Information related to archived, deleted, or restored conversations and other account-management actions.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the Service.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting support or submitting feedback.

We do not need every category above for every user. The information processed depends on the features you choose to use and the technical architecture of Friindzy.

03 — Account

Account Information

If you create a Friindzy account, we may process information required to create, authenticate, maintain, secure, and provide your account.

This may include your email address, display name, account identifier, authentication information, account preferences, and related administrative information.

We use account information to provide account functionality, maintain security, respond to support requests, and perform other purposes described in this Policy.

04 — Content

Chats, Prompts & User Content

When you use Friindzy to communicate with an AI model, the content you submit may include prompts, messages, instructions, code, questions, and other material.

If conversation history is enabled, information associated with your conversations may be stored so that you can access, organize, archive, restore, or delete your conversations through the available features.

You control what information you choose to place into your prompts and conversations. Do not submit highly sensitive or confidential information unless you understand how it will be processed by the relevant AI provider and Friindzy.

05 — Files

Files & Images

Friindzy may allow you to upload files, documents, images, source code, or other content for AI-assisted processing.

Files may be transmitted to or processed by the AI provider required to perform the feature you request. Processing can depend on the selected model, provider, file type, file size, context limits, and other technical restrictions.

You are responsible for ensuring that you have the right to upload and process the files you provide.

06 — BYOK

API Keys & Bring Your Own Key

Friindzy may provide a Bring Your Own Key (“BYOK”) system that allows you to connect API keys from supported AI providers.

API keys are authentication credentials belonging to the relevant provider account. Their use may result in requests being made to that provider under your account and may consume your provider quota or incur charges according to that provider's terms.

Friindzy may support multiple API keys so that the application can use another configured key when a limit is reached, subject to the provider, application logic, and availability.

API-key handling must match Friindzy's actual technical implementation. Before publishing this Policy, specify whether keys are stored locally on the device, encrypted on a server, or otherwise transmitted/stored. Do not publish a security claim that the implementation does not actually provide.

You should revoke or replace an API key immediately if you believe it has been exposed or compromised.

07 — Personalization

Custom Instructions

If you use Custom Instructions, the information you enter may be stored and used to personalize AI interactions according to the feature's design.

Custom Instructions may be included with requests sent to an AI provider when necessary to provide the requested functionality.

You should avoid placing passwords, private keys, financial account credentials, or other unnecessary sensitive information in Custom Instructions.

Technical & Usage Information

To operate and protect Friindzy, we may process technical information such as device information, operating-system information, application version, network information, timestamps, diagnostic information, crash information, and security-related events, where applicable to the actual implementation.

We may use this information to troubleshoot problems, detect abuse, maintain security, understand service reliability, and improve the Service.
