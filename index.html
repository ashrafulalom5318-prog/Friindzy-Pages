01 — About

A simple profile is fine. A story is better. This space is for explaining what you build, why you build it, and what keeps you curious.
I like turning rough ideas into things people can actually use.

I'm an independent developer who enjoys experimenting with software, AI, mobile applications, and new ways of building. Most projects start as a small idea and grow through a lot of testing, redesigning, and coding.

Friindzy is one of those projects — created around a simple problem: making AI-assisted development more accessible to developers who want to build without being blocked by expensive tools and limited usage.

I care about clean interfaces, useful features, and products that feel intentionally made rather than simply assembled.
