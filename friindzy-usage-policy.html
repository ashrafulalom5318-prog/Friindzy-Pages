01 — Purpose

Purpose of This Policy

This Usage Policy describes how Friindzy is intended to be used and provides practical rules for responsible use of its AI, coding, file, account, and API-related features.

This Policy works together with the Friindzy Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Security information, and applicable third-party provider policies.

Where a third-party provider has stricter requirements, your use of that provider through Friindzy must also comply with those requirements.

02 — Allowed

Allowed Use

Friindzy may be used for lawful and legitimate activities, including:

  • Learning, studying, research, and general information gathering.
  • Programming and software-development assistance.
  • Writing, editing, brainstorming, summarization, and translation.
  • Debugging and explaining source code.
  • Creating project documentation and technical notes.
  • Working with supported files and documents.
  • Generating images or other supported creative content.
  • Exploring ideas, planning projects, and improving productivity.
  • Other lawful uses that do not violate this Policy or applicable provider rules.
03 — Development

Coding & Software Development

Friindzy is designed to be useful for developers, including beginners, independent developers, students, and users working on personal or professional software projects.

You may use Friindzy for code generation, debugging, refactoring, explanations, documentation, project planning, and related development workflows.

You remain responsible for reviewing and testing generated code. AI-generated code may contain bugs, vulnerabilities, incorrect assumptions, incompatible dependencies, or licensing concerns.

Before deploying generated code: review it, test it, check dependencies, and verify security-sensitive functionality independently.

04 — AI

AI-Generated Content

AI Output should be treated as assistance rather than an automatic guarantee of accuracy.

AI models can produce incorrect, incomplete, outdated, biased, or misleading responses. You should verify important information before relying on it.

Do not use AI Output as a substitute for qualified professional advice where medical, legal, financial, safety, or other specialized professional judgment is required.

You are responsible for deciding whether and how to publish, distribute, execute, or otherwise use AI Output.

05 — BYOK

BYOK & API Usage

Friindzy may allow you to connect your own API keys from supported AI providers.

When using BYOK, requests made through Friindzy may consume the connected provider's quota, credits, rate limits, or other usage allowances.

  • Use only API keys you are authorized to use.
  • Keep API keys private and secure.
  • Do not share or publish API keys.
  • Follow the API provider's terms and usage policies.
  • Do not intentionally bypass provider limits or restrictions.
  • Monitor your provider account for unexpected usage.

If multiple API keys are configured, Friindzy may use another available key according to its implementation when a limit is reached. Provider availability and rate limits may still cause interruptions.

06 — Files

Files & Uploads

Friindzy may allow you to upload, read, create, modify, or process files as part of an AI-assisted task.

Upload only files that you have the legal right to use and process. Do not upload confidential, private, or sensitive information unless doing so is necessary and you understand the applicable processing.

Large contexts may be handled by organizing information into files or other structured formats where supported. File handling remains subject to technical and provider limitations.

07 — Prohibited

Prohibited Use

You must not use Friindzy to facilitate, encourage, or intentionally perform unlawful or harmful activity, including:

  1. Fraud, scams, phishing, impersonation, or deceptive schemes.
  2. Unauthorized access to accounts, systems, devices, networks, or services.
  3. Credential theft, API-key theft, or unauthorized collection of secrets.
  4. Creation or distribution of malware intended to harm or compromise systems.
  5. Unauthorized surveillance, stalking, or privacy violations.
  6. Harassment, threats, exploitation, or targeted abuse.
  7. Sexual exploitation or abuse of children.
  8. Non-consensual sexual or intimate content involving real people.
  9. Content or activity that violates applicable law.
  10. Infringement of another person's intellectual-property or other legal rights.
  11. Attempts to bypass security controls or usage restrictions.
  12. Abuse of third-party APIs, infrastructure, or authentication systems.
  13. Activities intended to disrupt Friindzy or another service.
08 — Protection

Abuse & Circumvention

You must not attempt to defeat or circumvent technical, security, account, or usage controls implemented by Friindzy or a third-party provider.

This includes attempts to:

  • Bypass authentication or access controls.
  • Evade rate limits or provider restrictions.
  • Use unauthorized accounts or credentials.
  • Exploit bugs or vulnerabilities for unauthorized purposes.
  • Interfere with service availability or reliability.
  • Use automation to abuse or overload the Service.

Security research should be conducted responsibly and without accessing, modifying, or exposing other users' information.

09 — Privacy

Privacy & Sensitive Information

You control much of the information you choose to place into prompts, conversations, files, and Custom Instructions.

Avoid unnecessarily submitting passwords, private keys, authentication tokens, financial credentials, highly sensitive personal information, or confidential information.

Before sending sensitive information through a third-party AI provider, review that provider's privacy policy and applicable API documentation.

Remember: Friindzy can provide the interface, but information sent to a third-party AI provider may be processed according to that provider's own systems and policies.

10 — Providers

Third-Party Provider Rules

Friindzy may connect you to multiple AI providers. Each provider may impose additional restrictions on models, content, API usage, automation, rate limits, and account activity.

You are responsible for complying with the applicable provider's requirements when using its API through Friindzy.

Friindzy cannot guarantee that a provider will accept a request, continue supporting a model, or maintain a particular API behavior.

11 — Limits

Usage Limits & Availability

Friindzy itself or connected providers may impose technical limits, including limits relating to requests, context size, files, model availability, API quotas, rate limits, or other resources.

Reaching a provider limit may result in a request being rejected, delayed, interrupted, or routed to another configured provider or API key where the feature supports that behavior.

Multiple API keys do not guarantee unlimited AI usage. Each provider and key remains subject to its own rules and limits.

12 — Reporting

Reporting Problems

If Friindzy provides a reporting, feedback, or flagging feature, you may use it to report problematic AI-generated content, abuse, bugs, or other issues.

When reporting an issue, avoid including unnecessary passwords, API keys, private credentials, or other sensitive information.

Security vulnerabilities should be reported privately through the security contact provided on the Friindzy Security page.

13 — Enforcement

Policy Enforcement

Friindzy may investigate suspected violations of this Policy and may take reasonable action to protect users, the Service, third-party providers, or the public.

Depending on the circumstances, action may include:

  • Blocking or restricting a request or feature.
  • Removing or restricting access to content where appropriate.
  • Restricting API or provider functionality.
  • Suspending or terminating an account.
  • Taking security measures to prevent further abuse.
  • Reporting activity to authorities where required or legally appropriate.

Enforcement decisions may depend on the severity, frequency, intent, context, and potential impact of the activity.

14 — Updates

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Usage Policy as Friindzy develops, new features are introduced, provider requirements change, or applicable laws and platform policies evolve.

The latest version will display an updated effective or “Last updated” date.
